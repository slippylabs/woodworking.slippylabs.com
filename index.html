WOODWORKING PLANS

Pick a build, set the dimensions you actually want, and get a dimensioned drawing, a cut list in real fractions, a board-by-board shopping list and steps you can print and take to the shop.

Every plan on this page is parametric: the drawings, the cut list and the price all recompute as you move a slider. Sizes are worked out in actual lumber dimensions — a 1x6 is 5-1/2" wide, not 6" — and where a slatted panel can only land on a whole number of boards, the page tells you the size you will really end up with instead of the one you asked for. Nothing is uploaded anywhere; it all runs in this tab.
